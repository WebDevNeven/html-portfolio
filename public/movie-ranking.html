The Best Movies According to Neven

My Top 3 Movies of All-Time

Creed (2015)

"Creed" follows Adonis Johnson, Apollo Creed's son, who seeks boxing training from Rocky Balboa. Together, they prepare Adonis for major fight, focusing on legacy and perservance.
"Creed" revitalizes the "Rocky" series with standout performances, intense boxing action, and a compelling story about legacy and determination.

Harry Potter - Franchise

The "Harry Potter" franchise follows young wizard Harry Potter as he attends Hogwarts School for Wizards, battles the dark wizard Voldemort, and grows into a hero alongside his friends.
The "Harry Potter" franchise captivates with its magical world, compelling characters, and epic battles between good and evil, making it a beloved and timeless adventure for all ages.

Troy

"Troy" follows the epic siege of Troy as Greek hero Achilles leads his forces against the Trojans, driven by love, honor, and destiny in a dramatic retelling of Homer's "The Iliad".
"Troy" impresses with its grand scale, thrilling battle scenes, and strong performances, offering a dramatic and engaging retteling of the classic epic.
